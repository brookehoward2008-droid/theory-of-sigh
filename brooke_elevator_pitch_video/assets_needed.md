# Assets Needed — B-roll, Overlays & Music

Editorial, warm, sincere. Favor real, slightly imperfect footage over glossy corporate stock.
Shallow depth of field, warm/natural light, ivory & wood tones, hands-at-work shots. Avoid
fake smiles, neon UI, and "business handshake" clichés.

**Free, student-friendly sources (CC0 / free license — credit where asked):**
Pexels · Unsplash · Pixabay · Coverr · Mixkit · Videvo · (Adobe Stock if you have access via school).
Keep a note of where each clip came from for your sources slide if required.

## TYPE (fonts)

- **Display / serif:** Crimson Pro, Cormorant Garamond, or Playfair Display (name, end card).
- **Sans / UI:** Inter, Bricolage Grotesque, or Helvetica/Arial/Liberation (labels, captions, chips).
- Free via Google Fonts. Install both before editing so titles render consistently.

---

## MUSIC (instrumental bed)

- Mood: warm, hopeful, understated — felt piano / soft keys / light ambient. No heavy beats.
- Length: ~70s (or loop a shorter cue). Level −24 to −20 LUFS, duck under VO.
- Sources: Pixabay Music, Mixkit, Uppbeat, YouTube Audio Library, Coverr Music, Canva audio.
- Search terms: `warm piano underscore`, `soft inspiring ambient`, `gentle felt piano`, `calm hopeful instrumental`.
- Optional SFX: one soft paper/whoosh transition (Mixkit) — use sparingly.
