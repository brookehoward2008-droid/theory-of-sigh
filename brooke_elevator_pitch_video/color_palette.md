# Color Palette — Brooke Howard Elevator Pitch

Editorial, warm, and quiet. Ivory and charcoal carry 90% of the frame; teal, gold,
and rose are used sparingly as accents (rules, labels, small blocks). Resist the
urge to use all five at once — pick one accent per scene.

| Role | Name | HEX | RGB | Where to use |
|------|------|-----|-----|--------------|
| Primary background | Warm Ivory | `#F5F1EA` | 245, 241, 234 | Main canvas, light cards, body of frame, paper texture |
| Primary text / dark bg | Deep Charcoal | `#2C2C2C` | 44, 44, 44 | Headlines on ivory, caption boxes, dark cards, end card |
| Accent 1 | Muted Teal | `#5D8A87` | 93, 138, 135 | Section labels, one full-bleed card, thin rules, underlines |
| Accent 2 | Soft Gold | `#C9A96E` | 201, 169, 110 | Hairline rules, kicker labels, small dividers, "active" accents |
| Accent 3 | Dusty Rose | `#B98B82` | 185, 139, 130 | Sparingly — a single keyword highlight, a soft block, hover state |

## Exact usage map

- **Canvas / pillarbox background** (behind the portrait talking-head): Warm Ivory `#F5F1EA`.
- **Lower-third + caption box**: Deep Charcoal `#2C2C2C` at **72% opacity**, text in Warm Ivory `#F5F1EA`.
- **Name (lower third headline)**: Deep Charcoal `#2C2C2C` on ivory, or Warm Ivory on the charcoal bar.
- **Title / college lines**: Deep Charcoal at ~80% (or `#4A4A4A`) for hierarchy under the name.
- **Section kicker labels** (e.g., "C A R E E R   E X P E R I E N C E"): Soft Gold `#C9A96E`, tracked caps.
- **Keyword chips** (Life Experience, etc.): Charcoal `#2C2C2C` chip @ 72%, Ivory text, optional 2px Gold underline.
- **Hairline rules / dividers**: Soft Gold `#C9A96E` (1–2px) or Muted Teal for heavier rules.
- **Full-bleed accent card** (one only — the "Work Ethic" card works well): Muted Teal `#5D8A87`, Ivory text.
- **End card**: Deep Charcoal `#2C2C2C` background, "Thank you" in Warm Ivory, a single Soft Gold rule.
- **Dusty Rose**: reserve for ONE moment — e.g., underline the word "creativity," or a soft block behind a single keyword. Do not spread it around.

## Contrast / accessibility notes

- Ivory `#F5F1EA` text on Charcoal `#2C2C2C` → contrast ratio ≈ **13.5:1** (excellent; this is the caption combo).
- Charcoal text on Ivory → ≈ **13.5:1** (excellent for headlines/body).
- Ivory text on Teal `#5D8A87` → ≈ **3.4:1** — large/bold text only (24px+ bold). Fine for big keywords, not for captions.
- Gold `#C9A96E` and Rose `#B98B82` are **accents, not body text** — use at 24px+ or as rules. Avoid gold text on ivory for anything small (low contrast).
- Captions must always sit on the charcoal box, never directly on ivory or teal, so they stay legible over any footage.

## Suggested type pairing (editorial)

- **Display / name / "Thank you"**: a refined serif — *Crimson Pro*, *Cormorant Garamond*, or *Playfair Display*.
- **Labels / captions / UI**: a clean humanist sans — *Inter*, *Bricolage Grotesque*, or *Liberation/Helvetica/Arial*.
- Tracked caps (letter-spacing ~200) for kicker labels; generous line spacing everywhere. Keep it airy.
